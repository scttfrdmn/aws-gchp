VERDICT:
  (1) TRANSPORT CORRECT + STABLE at scale -- byte-identical, 0 fallback, and crucially NO timeout/
      thrash (the C180 failure mode). The producer-side sidecar eliminated the per-object fork-storm.
  (2) OFF-NODE WALL = 2.7x on-node at C90. NOT a transport-producer problem (RUN=92% of baseline);
      the drag is the WORKER side (still per-object aws s3api, ~6 forks/slice) + per-superstep S3
      round-trip latency dominating C90's modest chem fraction. Consistent w/ prior finding:
      S3 decoupling = elasticity/provisioning/fault-tolerance, NOT raw speed, at C90.
  (3) C180 remains the open question: 74% chem fraction (vs C90's smaller) may amortize the handoff
      far better (on-node decoupling got 2.23x at C180). But the worker-side per-object aws would
      also drag C180 until it too is moved to a sidecar.
